## Changed

- The first Scan my Mac button uses Andromeda’s blue-to-purple gradient and expands into the scan checklist while scanning starts.
- Overview groups cleanup suggestions by folder type under Safe to remove and Review first. Expand a row to see what uses space and how to review it.
- Worth knowing starts collapsed. Expand it to see the storage explanations.
- Scan coverage explains why some locations can stay unreadable after granting Full Disk Access and what can contribute to unaccounted space.
- Overview groups cleanup suggestions by folder type. Expand a row to see what uses space and open its review.
- Worth knowing starts collapsed, and scan coverage explains unreadable locations and unaccounted space.
- Hover over or click the info icon on a cleanup row to see what happens after removal and how the item is removed.
- The Andromeda galaxy in the menu bar opens a storage drawer that slides in from the display’s right edge.

## Added

- Open Andromeda from the menu bar to see free space across mounted drives and the startup disk’s usage and history.
- Open Tools from the sidebar or menu bar to find App Leftovers, Uninstaller, Downloads, Duplicate Finder, Similar Images, Large and Old Files, System Junk, and Time Machine Snapshots.
- AI Clutter reviews AI app caches and logs, individual LM Studio GGUF files, and models listed by the local Ollama service.
- Mail Attachments reviews local downloads with verified matching copies in Apple Mail. Trash Bins reviews selected files on the startup disk and supported external drives before permanent deletion.
- Maintenance Tasks brings together startup disk verification, Spotlight reindexing, DNS cache flushing, and links to Apple Mail and Disk Utility. Spotlight and DNS use the updated optional helper.
- Review simulator devices by name, OS version, and status. Select individual devices or removable Xcode runtimes and review the selection before removing them.

## Fixed

- Runtime lists match mounted simulator images to their backing system assets and show the on-disk size where available.
- Clicking the menu-bar icon a second time closes the storage drawer.
